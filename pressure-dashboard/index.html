          What counts as pressure

          A ball is high-pressure when the result is still open and that ball can move it far more than a typical ball. The top quarter of balls qualify.

          Four in ten chase balls are high-pressure, against one in ten in the first innings

          View as table

          Pressure Index = leverage × (1 + jeopardy) × occasion

          Leverage
          How far the next ball can swing win probability, against an average ball. It rises as a live chase runs out of balls.
          Jeopardy
          How much more a wicket costs than usual at that stage, from 0 to 1. It captures the collapse, in either innings.
          Occasion
          1.25 in knockouts, otherwise 1.
